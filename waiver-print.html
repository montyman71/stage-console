3. Terms & Conditions of Release
  1. Express Assumption of Risk: I voluntarily acknowledge that competitive tournament fishing, boating navigation, and related activities carry inherent and unanticipated hazards. These include, but are not limited to: collision with submerged hazards, rough water conditions, extreme weather, lightning strikes, hypothermia, drowning, equipment failure, and vehicular or launch ramp incidents. I expressly assume full responsibility for all risks of injury, disability, death, or property loss.
  2. Covenant Not to Sue & Liability Release: In consideration of participating in this AAFL-sanctioned event, I hereby release, waive, discharge, and hold harmless the American Anglers Fishing League (AAFL), its officers, directors, sponsors, tournament directors, committee staff, and host marina facilities from any and all liability, claims, demands, damages, or causes of action arising out of negligence, personal injury, or property damage sustained during or in connection with the event.
  3. Safe Boating Compliance & Safety Equipment: I certify that whenever the combustible engine is in gear above idle speed, all boat occupants will wear a securely fastened, Coast Guard-approved chest-type personal flotation device (PFD), and the vessel engine kill switch lanyard will be physically attached to the operator. I further agree to observe all state, federal, and local navigation and boating laws.
  4. Polygraph / Truth Verification Examination: I agree to submit to a polygraph or truth verification examination at any time upon request of the AAFL Tournament Committee. Refusal to submit or failure to pass shall result in immediate disqualification and forfeiture of any prize purse, trophies, and season points.
  5. Media & Likeness Release: I hereby grant AAFL and its official sponsors the unrestricted right to photograph, record, broadcast, and publish my name, image, voice, statistics, and tournament results without additional notice or compensation.
